# Name and logo

The KinShip source code is licensed under [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). That license does **not** cover the KinShip name or the logo (the sailboat with a leaf sail, and the "KinShip" wordmark). They identify the official project.

## What you may do

- Run, study, change and share the software under the AGPL.
- Say truthfully that your software is based on, compatible with or a fork of KinShip.
- Use the name when referring to the official project, for example in articles, tutorials and support requests.

## What you may not do

- Use the KinShip name or logo for a modified version in a way that suggests it is the official project or endorsed by it.
- Publish a fork or modified copy under the name "KinShip" or with the official logo.

If you publish a modified version, please give it a different name and your own logo.

The AGPL still requires anyone who runs a modified copy as a network service to offer its users the source of their changes. That is separate from this note.
