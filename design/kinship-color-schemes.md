# KinShip color schemes (Nov 14, 2026)

Companion to `kinship-v2-requirements.md`. JC found the original green too strong (the page reads dark green in dark mode, and pale green in light mode behind callouts, selected rows and photo placeholders).

**Decided by JC:** keep **all four** schemes so each person can choose their own. Also offer the choice in the first-run setup.

## Schemes
Each has a light and a dark version. Light or Dark still follows the device unless the person picks one.

| Scheme | Light page / accent | Dark page / accent | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forest** (original) | cream #f6f4ec, deep teal #1e4d4a, green tint #e0ede1 | #0e1b19, leaf green #8cc488 | The logo kit's palette |
| **Warm sand** | #f4efe4, teal #1e4d4a, sand tint #ece4d1 | #1b1812, light teal #7fc1b8 | Keeps the brand teal, drops the green wash |
| **Slate blue** | #f3f5f9, blue #2f4b7c, tint #e1e8f3 | #0f1520, #8fb0e8 | Cool, calm |
| **Graphite** | #f4f4f2, #2a2e35, tint #e7e8ea | #111214, #c9ccd2 | Neutral |

Status colors (Active, Living, Done, warnings, errors) are the same in every scheme, so green still means "good" even in Slate or Graphite.

## Where the choice lives
- **My settings → Display → Color scheme.** Per person, applies to that person only.
- **First-run setup, step 2 (Tree):** four swatches under "Pick your colors", applied right away. Everyone can change it later.
- The mockup also has a **Colors** dropdown in the yellow preview bar.
- Remembered in the browser in the mockup. In the real app it would be saved on the account.
