Everything a family archive needs, shown in the order it ships

Every feature carries the release that brings it, so you always know what works today and what is coming.

Available nowIn progressPlannedRelease numbers match the roadmap.

People and relationships

0.2.0

Partners, children, maiden and married surnames, custom labels, and step and half relatives worked out for you. Add a relative from a card with plus buttons. Flexible dates, including partial ones.

Backup and restore

0.2.0

Encrypted backups with a test button and a command line, plus an automatic safety copy before any database change. In-app Restore arrives in 0.3.

Update notices

0.2.0

Your SuperAdmin sees what is new, with a warning to back up first when a release changes the database. Nothing about your install is sent, and the check can be switched off.

Chart views

0.3

Pedigree, Fan and Descendants alongside the Family tree, with siblings' partners drawn, an option for aunts, uncles and cousins, and a chart that opens centered on you.

Relationship finder

0.3

How two people are related, with cousin levels, half, step and in-law names, and a plain explanation of "once removed".

Search, timeline and map

0.3

Find anyone fast, see a Memory of the day and who is coming up, browse a timeline, and place events on an OpenStreetMap map.

Photos and files

0.3

Profile photos and media upload. Every file is encrypted by the app before it is stored.

Email and sign-in

0.4

Invites and password resets by email. OpenID Connect single sign-on (any standards-compliant provider, tested with Authentik) with roles set by group, enforced while it is on. Authenticator-app sign-in for SuperAdmins, and an in-app bell with birthday and remembrance reminders.

Sources and research

0.5

Sources, citations and reliability, a Places library, stories, a duplicate finder with merge and 30-day undo, and a history of who changed what. Optional automatic transcription of handwritten scans, off until an admin connects an engine.

Health history

0.6

Conditions and surgeries, private by default and shared person by person, enforced by Postgres row-level security. A family health report you can take to a doctor.

Reports, import and export

0.7

Life summary, family group sheet and outlines, printable or as PDF. GEDCOM 5.5.1 import with a preview and export that shows living people as "Living".

DNA

1.2 and 1.3

A DNA tab on each profile, haplogroup lines, then match lists checked against your tree. Raw files stay private and encrypted.

The right access for each person

Four roles keep the tree safe without making anyone ask for permission twice.

SuperAdmin

Runs the installation: accounts, backups, storage and update checks.

Family Admin

Edits everything in the tree and invites Members and Users.

Member

Edits their own profile and their descendants, and adds sources.

User

Reads the tree and photos. Nothing to break, and a good account for relatives who only want to look.
