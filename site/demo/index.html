This account can look at everything but cannot change anything. You start at Charles Darwin.

The demo resets every night

At about 08:00 UTC the whole demo is wiped and rebuilt from the same data, and it is offline for about a minute. Please do not enter anything real: the sign-in above is shared with everyone.

What you are looking at

  • The demo runs release 0.2.0. Charts such as Pedigree, Fan and Descendants, and photos, arrive in 0.3, so they are not in it yet.
  • The people are public historical figures. Names, years and relationships come from Wikipedia. Most dates are years only.
  • It is the same software you would install, on a small server. Nothing is mocked.
